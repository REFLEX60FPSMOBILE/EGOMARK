The platform

Built to be driven by your own code

The same catalog, stock and delivery as the store, exposed to resellers through a REST API and a webhook.

  • Full catalog access

    Every product in the store, with its variants, through a single endpoint.

    GET /products

  • Reseller prices

    The price the API returns is your cost, with your tier discount already applied. You set your own selling price.

    GET /balance

  • Live stock

    The stock you see is the stock you can order, per variant. null means unlimited.

    "stock": 37

  • API and automation

    Create orders from a script, a backend or a bot. Charged to your balance, delivered in the same response.

    POST /orders

  • Order management

    Every order with its keys or file, paginated. Social orders from the followers panel are included.

    GET /orders?page=N

  • External stores

    Use your webhook URL as the dynamic delivery URL of your SellAuth store. One URL covers your whole catalog.

    /api/reseller/webhook/<token>

SellAuth dynamic delivery

POST /api/reseller/webhook/<token>
  1. 01

    Your SellAuth store

    A buyer pays for a product set to Dynamic delivery.

  2. 02

    EGO Market webhook

    We verify the signature, charge your balance and pick the credential.

  3. 03

    Delivered by SellAuth

    The credential comes back as plain text and SellAuth shows it to the buyer.

  • Every call is signed with your SellAuth webhook secret and checked on arrival.
  • A retry of the same invoice item returns the first answer and is never charged twice.
  • Nothing is charged for an undeliverable product, and short deliveries are refunded to your balance.
